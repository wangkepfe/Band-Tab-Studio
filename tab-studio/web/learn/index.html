🎓 Ear Trainer

You hear a key established, then one note. Say which scale degree it is — 1, ♭3, 5 — not which letter. Degrees are the same in every key and in every octave, which is why this is the one ear skill that transfers to playing.

The ladder

Level 1 is 1 2 3 and lights three keys. Level 7 is all twelve. Aim for about 80%: under 70% drop a level, over 90% move up. Eight to twelve minutes a day beats an hour once a week.

The microphone is optional

Everything works without it. If you turn it on it is push-to-sing — live only while you hold the button (or Shift), never between presses. No audio is ever recorded, stored or sent anywhere; the pitch is worked out in this page and thrown away.
